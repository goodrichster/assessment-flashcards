## Step-by-Step Solutions (Selected)

### HG-06 — Supplementary Angles
1. Supplementary angles sum to 180°.
2. 3x + (2x + 20) = 180
3. 5x + 20 = 180 → 5x = 160 → **x = 32**

### HG-12 — Same-Side Interior
1. Same-side interior angles are supplementary.
2. 4x + 2x = 180
3. 6x = 180 → **x = 30**

### HG-17 — SAS
1. Identify two corresponding sides.
2. Identify the included angle (between those sides).
3. Apply Side-Angle-Side (SAS) congruence.

### HG-24 — Distance
1. Distance formula: d = √[(x₂−x₁)² + (y₂−y₁)²]
2. d = √[(4−1)² + (6−2)²] = √[9 + 16] = √25 = **5**

### HG-25 — Perpendicular Slopes
1. Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals.
2. Slope 3 → perpendicular slope = **−1/3**
